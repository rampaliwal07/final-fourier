Theory: Fourier & Harmonic Series

Fourier Series

Any periodic signal can be expressed as sum of sines and cosines: f(x)=a₀ + Σ (aₙ cos(nx) + bₙ sin(nx)).

Harmonic series: fₙ = n·f₀ (fundamental frequency multiples). First harmonic = fundamental.

Formulas

a₀ = 1/(2π) ∫₋π^π f(x)dx, aₙ = 1/π ∫ f(x)cos(nx)dx, bₙ = 1/π ∫ f(x)sin(nx)dx.

Applications: Signal processing, audio, communications, vibrations.

📐 Key Formulas

Fourier Coefficients (discrete approx): aₙ = (1/L) Σ y·cos(nπx/L), bₙ = (1/L) Σ y·sin(nπx/L)
Period L = (x_max - x_min)/2  |  f(x) ≈ a₀/2 + Σ [aₙ cos(nπx/L) + bₙ sin(nπx/L)]

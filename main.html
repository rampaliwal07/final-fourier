Theory: Fourier Series & Harmonics

📐 Fourier Series Representation

For a periodic function f(x) with period 2L: f(x) = a₀/2 + Σ[ aₙ cos(nπx/L) + bₙ sin(nπx/L) ] (n=1 to ∞).

📏 Fourier Coefficients

a₀ = (1/L)∫ f(x) dx, aₙ = (1/L)∫ f(x) cos(nπx/L) dx, bₙ = (1/L)∫ f(x) sin(nπx/L) dx.
For discrete data: a₀ ≈ (2∑y)/(4L), aₙ ≈ (2/L)∑ y·cos(nπx/L), bₙ ≈ (2/L)∑ y·sin(nπx/L).

🎵 Harmonic Concept

First harmonic (fundamental) frequency ω, second 2ω, etc. Fourier series extracts harmonic amplitudes.

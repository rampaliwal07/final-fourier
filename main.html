Objective of harmonic analysis

🎯 Experiment Objective

To understand Harmonic Analysis in Fourier Series: representing any periodic waveform as sum of sine and cosine harmonics. Students will compute Fourier coefficients (a₀, aₙ, bₙ) from discrete data points, visualize the Fourier series approximation, and verify the constant term a₀.

  • Derive Fourier coefficients from tabulated data.
  • Interpret role of fundamental and higher harmonics.
  • Validate results using an interactive simulation.

Theory: Fourier Series & Harmonics

📐 Fourier Series Representation

For a periodic function f(x) with period 2L, the Fourier series is:
f(x) = a₀/2 + Σ[ aₙ cos(nπx/L) + bₙ sin(nπx/L) ] (n=1 to ∞).

📏 Fourier Coefficients (Euler formulas)

a₀ = (1/L) ∫₋ˡˡ f(x) dx    aₙ = (1/L) ∫₋ˡˡ f(x) cos(nπx/L) dx    bₙ = (1/L) ∫₋ˡˡ f(x) sin(nπx/L) dx

For discrete data (numerical harmonic analysis):
a₀ ≈ (2∑y)/(4L)   aₙ ≈ (2/L)∑ y·cos(nπx/L),   bₙ ≈ (2/L)∑ y·sin(nπx/L).

🎵 Harmonic Concept

First harmonic (fundamental) has frequency ω, second harmonic 2ω, etc. Fourier series extracts amplitude of each harmonic from given samples, crucial in signal processing and vibrations.
